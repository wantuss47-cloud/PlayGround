FIELD GUIDE 01—06

Traction is a conversation
with the ground.

TAIGA is a slow, systemic off-road simulation. The terrain remembers every pass; the truck remembers every mistake.

DESIGN PILLAR 01WEIGHT · GRIP · CONSEQUENCE
SIMULATION SPEC 01 / DRIVETRAIN

Torque, not horsepower.

A lightweight aggregate vehicle model turns terrain, traction, slip, range, and upgrades into vehicle-speed response. It does not simulate individual wheel suspension or wheel-by-wheel drivetrain forces.

01

Powertrain

Throttle, speed, traction, and bogged-load estimates drive acceleration, RPM, and engine/clutch heat. Selectable high/low range changes the response; 4×4 and upgrade state modify aggregate traction. Gear display is a simplified readout, not a component-by-component gearbox simulation.

ENGINE / CLUTCH HEAT · STALL / RESTART
02

Drivetrain upgrades

Portal axles, transfer case, lockers, and shafts are represented as build upgrades that adjust vehicle stats or unlock controls. There is no wheel-speed parity, shaft-stress, or hard-turn binding simulation. Differential lock is a single aggregate traction toggle.

UPGRADE TREE · 4×4 · HIGH / LOW · DIFF LOCK
03

Tyres & pressure

When CTIS is installed, switch between 30 and 18 PSI. Lower pressure and tyre upgrades adjust aggregate traction on soft ground; slip gradually wears the tyre condition. Individual tyre deformation, punctures, bead failure, and heat are not modeled.

30 PSI · 18 PSI · AGGREGATE SLIP / WEAR
04

Terrain & water

Driving and wheelspin accumulate persistent rut values in terrain cells; visible tracks and changed traction, drag, and surface readings follow. Water depth affects engine condition and can stall the engine beyond the intake limit. A snorkel raises that limit; sealed ignition reduces crossing wear.

RUTS · TERRAIN RESPONSE · WATER STALL / RESTART
05

Damage & maintenance

Engine, chassis, and tyre condition are aggregate meters. Slip, rough ground, deep water, and overheating can wear them; high engine or clutch temperatures reduce acceleration, with severe heat adding engine wear. Paid field repair restores some condition; there are no separately simulated axle or suspension failures.

ENGINE · CHASSIS · TYRES · TEMPERATURES
06

Recovery

The winch selects a nearby road-map point as its anchor and pulls the vehicle toward it; the upgraded winch increases reach and pull. Emergency recovery relocates the scout to the nearest road point for ₽500. Cable snag, line angle, anchor breakage, battery draw, and cargo loss are not simulated.

WINCH · ROAD ANCHOR · ₽500 EMERGENCY RECOVERY
